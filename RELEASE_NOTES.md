# Road Rash 64 Recompiled 1.4.5

- **Smoother racing:** improved terrain/scenery preparation and renderer buffer reuse reduce hitches. Testing at 60 FPS stayed smooth across multiple races with maximum draw distance, including Insanity; results depend on hardware and track.
- **Rival motorcycle audio:** more reliable engine playback across the opponent field, including Dumotos and passing bikes after a crash.
- **Presentation fixes:** improved shadows and restoration from crash highlights to race results.
- **Controls layout:** larger, evenly spaced setup panels keep all four players together.
- **More Characters 2.1.0:** 25 optional rider skins, including Gordon Freeman, all four Ninja Turtles, Shredder, Terminator, Judge Dredd, Alyx, Locust Grub, Lara Croft, BloodRayne, Duke Nukem and Serious Sam, with revised skin alignment.

## Downloads

Choose Windows or experimental native Linux. **More Characters** and the **optional texture mod** are separate downloads installed through Settings → Mods.

Supply your own supported **Road Rash 64 USA v1.0 ROM**. MK64 importing also requires your own supported **Mario Kart 64 USA ROM**. Keep your saves and settings when updating.

MK64 tracks, items and online play remain experimental. Online players need matching **1.4.5 / protocol 68** builds and imported packs.

Draw Distance at 100% can load the entire map, including areas used by other races. **50–60%** remains a good starting point on slower hardware and in multiplayer.
